The dedicated Yestream TV app ships as a ready-to-install IPK package (`com.yestream.tv.prototype_0.25.6_all.ipk`). It targets LG webOS 5.x+ (Chromium 68+) and has been verified on LG OLED BX and newer webOS smart TVs.

<ContentDisclaimer />

## What's new in v0.25.6

- **Split-Screen Playlists Management**: Replaces legacy sources with a modern dual-pane layout.
- **Quick Connect QR Card**: Scan the TV screen with your smartphone camera to log directly into your customer self-service portal (`https://yestream.ai.studio/customer/login`) to manage playlists without remote-control typing.
- **Instant Cloud Sync**: One-click **Sync Cloud Playlists** button connects to Yestream Cloud (`/api/v1/devices/register` & `/api/v1/device/sync`) with automatic token renewal and 401 recovery.
- **Dual-Tab Playlist Modal**: Easily configure **Xtream Codes API** or **M3U Playlists** directly from your TV remote.
- **Account & Device Diagnostics**: View live hardware MAC address, 6-digit Device Key, and active license/trial expiration dates in **Settings → Account & Device**.
- **Expiration Guard**: Full-screen lock screen when your trial or license expires with one-click status verification.

## Enable Developer Mode and pair the TV

1. On the TV, install **Developer Mode** from LG Apps. Sign in with your LG Developer account, enable **Dev Mode Status**, and let the TV restart.
2. On a computer on the same home network, [install LG’s webOS CLI](https://webostv.developer.lge.com/develop/tools/cli-installation). Run `ares-setup-device`, choose **add**, and save `living-room-tv` with the TV’s IP address, port `9922`, SSH user `prisoner`, and a blank password.
3. Reopen Developer Mode on the TV and turn on **Key Server**. Run `ares-novacom --device living-room-tv --getkey` and enter the 6-character passphrase shown on the TV.

> **macOS OpenSSH Tip**: Modern macOS OpenSSH disables older `ssh-rsa` key types by default. If `ares-install` fails with `no matching host key type found`, add the following to your `~/.ssh/config`:
> ```ssh-config
> Host 192.168.*.*
>     HostKeyAlgorithms +ssh-rsa
>     PubkeyAcceptedKeyTypes +ssh-rsa
> ```

## Install and launch

[Download the IPK](https://github.com/logicatuk/Yestream-pro/releases/download/v0.25.6/com.yestream.tv.prototype_0.25.6_all.ipk). From your terminal, run:

```sh
ares-install --device living-room-tv com.yestream.tv.prototype_0.25.6_all.ipk
ares-launch --device living-room-tv com.yestream.tv.prototype
```

## Quick Start on the TV

1. Open **Yestream TV** on your LG Smart TV.
2. Navigate to **Playlists** in the top navigation bar.
3. On the left pane, note your **MAC Address** and **Device Key**, or scan the dynamic **Quick Connect QR code** with your phone.
4. On your smartphone, log in and add your M3U playlist or Xtream Codes subscription.
5. Back on your TV, click **↻ Sync Cloud Playlists** to load your channels, movies, and series instantly!

Remember to press **EXTEND** in the LG Developer Mode app before the session timer expires.
