Yestream is a cross-platform IPTV player that brings all your playlists together in one beautiful app.

LIVE TV & EPG
- M3U/M3U8 playlist support
- Xtream Codes API integration (live, VOD, series, catch-up)
- Stalker portal support
- Full EPG guide with 7-day schedule

MOVIES & SERIES
- Xtream VOD catalog with categories
- Series with season/episode browser

CLOUD SYNC & MULTI-DEVICE
- Free 7-day trial per device
- Activate via QR code on yestream.ai.studio
- Playlists sync across devices

REMOTE & ACCESSIBILITY
- Full remote control support
- Parental lock (PIN)
